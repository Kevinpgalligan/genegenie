Citation Stats

This is a breakdown of what sources are available to support various entities in the tree, like people, marriages, and events.

The golden source of truth is a written record like a census. Next is recollections, first-hand accounts, family records and the like. Not good: family trees from public websites, since these are known to be thrown together haphazardly (e.g. someone might mistakenly add a whole branch to their tree because of a name matching one of their known ancestors). Finally, worse than that is to have no source at all.

People

Supporting evidence for the existence of people. Any source supporting an event in someone's life (like birth), or an event in their relationships, or a description of them, etc... will count towards the existence of that person. Everyone should have at least some evidence for their existence, otherwise why would they be in the tree!
